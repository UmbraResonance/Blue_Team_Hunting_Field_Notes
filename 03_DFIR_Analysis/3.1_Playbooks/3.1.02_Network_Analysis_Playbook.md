# Network Forensics Playbook (Universal)

> **Goal:** Reconstruct attack chains through traffic analysis to identify initial access, C2 communication, and data exfiltration.
> **Role:** SOC Analyst.
> **Methodology:** Descriptive → Diagnostic → Predictive → Prescriptive.
> **Output:** True Source/Attacker IP, Malicious Payloads, C2 Domains/IPs, and Exfiltration Volume.

---

## Strategic Approach: The Mindset
*Tactical entry points to isolate threats before performing deep-packet inspection:*

* **Initial Protocol Audit:** Start with common internet-facing protocols (HTTP/S, FTP, Email) to clear baseline noise.
* **Host-to-Host Traffic:** Monitor for internal workstation-to-workstation communication. Direct traffic between endpoints is rare and often indicates lateral movement.
* **Pattern Identification:** Search for "Beaconing" profiles characterized by connections at fixed time intervals.
* **Anomaly Detection:** Identify behavioral shifts, unusual User-Agent strings, or rare port bindings on a host.

---

## Phase 1: Descriptive Analysis (Triage & Scoping)
*Determine the environment baseline and identify "Top Talkers" before deep-packet inspection.*

### 1. Issue Definition & Scoping
* **Action:** Determine if the issue is a suspected breach or a network error.
* **Scope:** Define the target subnet (e.g., `192.168.100.0/24`), the timeframe, and specific target protocols.
* **Persistence:** If the issue is low-frequency (like slow C2), maintain the capture for an extended period.

### 2. Protocol Hierarchy & Statistics
* **Action:** Identify the distribution of protocols to spot anomalies (e.g., unexpected FTP or SMB in a DMZ).
* **Tool Vault Link:** Use [`6.08_Network_Traffic_Analysis`](../06_Tool_Command_Vault/6.08_Network_Traffic_Analysis.md) for Tshark summary commands.
* **Filter:** Wireshark -> Statistics -> Protocol Hierarchy.

### 3. Conversation & Load Analysis
* **Action:** Identify sessions with high data volume that may indicate exfiltration.
* **Tool Vault Link:** Execute `tshark -z conv,ip` to find Top Talkers.
* **Logic:** If $A \rightarrow B$ bytes are significantly higher than $B \rightarrow A$, investigate for unauthorized data transfer.

---

## Phase 2: Diagnostic Analysis (Attack Vector & Payload)
*Capture, filter, and understand the traffic to validate the cause of the problem.*

### 1. HTTP/Web Audit
* **Recon Check:** Use [`1.4_Network_Protocol_Filters`](../01_Hunting_Cheatsheets/1.4_Network_Protocol_Filters.csv) to filter `http.response.code == 404` for directory brute-forcing.
* **Exploit Check:** Filter for `POST` requests to `.php` or `.jsp` files to find webshell uploads.
* **Tool Check:** Search for automated scanners in `http.user_agent` (e.g., `sqlmap`, `nmap`).

### 2. Authentication & Brute Force
* **Action:** Monitor for credential harvesting or brute force attacks.
* **Filter:** Use `ftp.response.code == 530` for failed logins followed by `230` for success.
* **Documentation:** Record targeted usernames in [`7.2_Evidence_Artifact_Log`](../07_Reporting_Templates/7.2_Evidence_Artifact_Log.csv).

### 3. Artifact Recovery (Carving)
* **Action:** Reconstruct files transferred over HTTP, SMB, or FTP.
* **Tool Vault Link:** Use [`6.08_Network_Traffic_Analysis`](../06_Tool_Command_Vault/6.08_Network_Traffic_Analysis.md) for **NetworkMiner** extraction or Wireshark `Export Objects`.
* **Integrity:** Calculate SHA256 immediately using [`6.01_General_DFIR_Commands`](../06_Tool_Command_Vault/6.01_General_DFIR_Commands.md).

---

## Phase 3: Predictive Analysis (C2 & Risk Evaluation)
*Evaluate current findings to identify trends and deviations from expected values.*

### 1. C2 & Beaconing Detection
* **DNS Audit:** Check for long subdomains or TXT records using `dns.qry.type == 16`.
* **TLS Inspection:** Use `tls.handshake.extensions_server_name` (SNI) to identify encrypted C2 domains.
* **Jitter Analysis:** Check for consistent "Time Delta" between packets to confirm automated beacons.

### 2. Mind Mapping & Timeline
* **Action:** Annotate timeframes, suspicious hosts, and specific packet numbers for malicious conversations.
* **Timeline Tracker:** Log all attacker actions in [`7.1_Timeline_Tracker`](../07_Reporting_Templates/7.1_Timeline_Tracker.csv).

---

## Phase 4: Prescriptive Analysis (Containment & Hardening)
*Determine required actions to resolve the problem and prevent future recurrence.*

### 1. Containment & Reporting
* **Artifact Documentation:** Ensure all malicious IPs, Domains, and Hashes are in [`7.2_Evidence_Artifact_Log`](../07_Reporting_Templates/7.2_Evidence_Artifact_Log.csv).
* **Communication:** Summarize findings clearly to enable decisions on host quarantine or incident response.

### 2. Strategic Hardening & Lessons Learned
* **Detection Engineering:** Convert found TTPs into rules within [`02_Detection_Rules`](../02_Detection_Rules/).
* **Verification:** Cross-reference findings with host-side logs (e.g., Sysmon) using [`1.1_Master_Hunting_Matrix`](../01_Hunting_Cheatsheets/1.1_Master_Hunting_Matrix.csv).
* **Reflection:** Document what was done correctly, what failed, and how to improve. Update asset inventories and network maps.
